# Store listing draft

Preparation for [#57](https://github.com/utrost/Textr/issues/57). Updated for 0.5-alpha preparation; not submitted or published. Owner review is required for account declarations, distribution countries, audience, content rating and final declarations.

The release-specific English/German name, short description and full description are maintained in [Fastlane metadata](../../fastlane/metadata/android/) and validated with `python3 scripts/validate-store-metadata.py`. Use those committed files for the matching version instead of duplicating text here. They cover the current roadmap implementation, its limits, separate-copy policy and local recovery.

Developer: **utrost**. Privacy/support contact: [public Textr issues](https://github.com/utrost/Textr/issues). Privacy URL: https://github.com/utrost/Textr/blob/main/docs/privacy.md . Do not attach private documents to public issues.

## Submission fields still requiring owner input

- Developer and public issue contact are confirmed above. Play may additionally require account-specific verified contact fields; the owner must provide those directly in the console. No email or legal identity is invented.
- Suggested category: Tools. Confirm in the console.
- No ads, no login, no purchases, no restricted app access in the current build.
- Complete the rating questionnaire based on a local document viewer, not the arbitrary contents of user-supplied files. Do not invent an IARC rating.
- Choose intended audience, countries and distribution terms explicitly; this repository does not declare a child-targeted audience.
- Review [Data safety draft](data-safety.md), [privacy policy](../privacy.md), and the unresolved real-device/accessibility checks before upload.
